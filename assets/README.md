# README emblem

`ootmm-decrypted.png` combines the left half of the Ocarina of Time with the right half of Majora's Mask. Both halves are scaled proportionally and positioned so their visible top and bottom edges meet at the cut. The background is transparent.

Artwork sources, hosted by Zelda Wiki:

- [Ocarina of Time render](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/File:OoT_Ocarina_of_Time_Render.png), credited there to Zelda Europe; uploaded by Dany36.
- [Majora's Mask render](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/File:MM_Majora%27s_Mask_Render.png), retouched by HelmarokKing; the original upload credits Steven2113 and MARKOL.
